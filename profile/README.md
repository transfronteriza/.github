**Who we are.** A youth-led nonprofit registered in Spain's National Register of Associations (2026). We have city groups across Spain and work on both shores of the Strait of Gibraltar.

**What we do.** Community programmes, education and culture for young people, including migrants and young women: living together, diversity, equality and human rights.

**What we build here**

| Project | What it is | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **Website** | The WordPress theme of transfronteriza.net, deployed from GitHub | Live |
| **La Frontera** | A safe online forum for young people on free software: email access, anonymity and careful moderation | In design, 2026–2027 |

**How we work.** Free software first, privacy by design, and safeguarding for members aged 14 to 17. Nothing reaches `main` without review.

**Want to help?** Designers, developers and moderators are welcome as volunteers. Write to hola@transfronteriza.net.
